MoodPath Live

Turn on the camera and press Play. It reads your expression every fraction of a second, places you on the map, and the music follows: it meets you a step lighter than you feel (never angrier or sadder than you), then leads you toward the target, but never more than a step ahead. If you don't follow, it waits. If you get worse, it comes back to meet you, still a step lighter. Balance picks the direction for you: tense → calm, low → gently brighter. The feed on the right says why.
